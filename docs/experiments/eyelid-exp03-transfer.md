# Experiment 3: public-to-local eyelid classification

This independent experiment starts from verified Experiment 2 commit `f4b2cb5`. Experiment 1 and 2 artifacts, tags, branches and worktrees remain immutable. The owner requested public neural pretraining, local head and late-layer adaptation, the same 119-subject outer folds, and a separate nested LAB threshold analysis on 2026-10-11.

## Input and label contract

Read-only `--source-root` points to the authoritative AIMA checkout and `--public-root` to its public archive. Outputs use a new Git-ignored Experiment 3 directory under the source root. No original images, masks or metadata are modified or copied to Git.

The archive contains 218 original images and primary manually segmented palpebral PNGs. Masks are recovered using validated alpha support or the documented exact-white-background exceptions. Subject identity is country plus patient number. Source and decoded duplicates fail closed; conservative perceptual similarity groups stay together in public train/validation. Cross-domain similarity requires resolution before training.

The [source study](https://iris.cnr.it/retrieve/670c5fcb-ffd7-47c2-b3e5-81d55c27f886/1-s2.0-S0933365722002299-main.pdf) documents laboratory Hb in g/dL, Samsung S6 capture with controlled white LED illumination and a common distance, and a historical single cutoff of <=10.5 g/dL. That cutoff is not the local AIMA target. Public labels are derived explicitly from source Hb using unadjusted adult WHO thresholds: men <13, women <12 g/dL, ages 15–65. The [WHO 2024 guideline](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK602183/?report=reader) has different pregnancy thresholds. Public pregnancy is unrecorded; women with 10.5<=Hb<12 are excluded because their binary status can depend on pregnancy. This conservative harmonization follows the owner's instruction to resolve label compatibility without guessing. The owner's non-pregnancy attestation applies to local AIMA, not public patients. Local membership and labels remain unchanged.

Twenty public records lack usable Hb or fall outside 15–65, and 15 more have pregnancy-dependent labels. The expected eligible public cohort is 183. Workbook ancillary notes and nonempty trailing cells are preserved in the private audit, never silently treated as additional patient rows or clinical fields. No altitude/smoking adjustments are inferred.

ICC profiles are actually converted to sRGB with relative-colorimetric rendering. For a DNG whose oriented embedded JPEG pixels exactly equal its annotation TIFF, the JPEG's real profile supersedes the misleading TIFF tag. Other local TIFFs use their recorded profile. Untagged public JPEGs are explicitly assumed sRGB; this is not camera calibration. Public JPEG EXIF orientation is applied once by Pillow and checked against the independently validated OpenCV raster and mask geometry before resize. Public images are aligned to their mask resolution using area interpolation. No local mask is regenerated.

Both domains then use zero-margin masked tissue bounding boxes, ImageNet-mean background before interpolation, aspect-preserving bilinear antialiased resize and mean letterboxing to 224, and fixed ImageNet normalization. Glare remains. Training-only horizontal flips preserve tissue color; no color enhancement or learned normalization is fitted. Mixed local export histories, unknown pilot settings, missing public profiles, public controlled illumination versus local capture, and geographic/age/sex/prevalence differences remain domain limitations. The original six LAB features remain unchanged for the separately versioned threshold comparator.

## Implemented neural training

`src/eyelid_transfer_neural.py` implements MobileNetV3-Small from the exact cached ImageNet1K_V1 checkpoint, global average pooling (576), dropout 0.2 and a binary linear head. Public pretraining adapts the head and final two backbone blocks; earlier weights and all BatchNorm running statistics remain frozen. Local A adapts the public-initialized head only; B adapts the head and the same late blocks at a smaller learning rate. AdamW uses weight decay 0.001, batch size 32 and BCE positive weights computed from each training partition. Public rates are head 0.001 / late blocks 0.0001; A head 0.001; B head 0.0003 / late blocks 0.00001. Public training allows 24 epochs, local inner fits 12, with validation AP selection, weighted-BCE tie-breaking and patience 4. Outer refits use the rounded median inner best epoch count and receive no outer validation labels.

Frozen early-layer maps for original and horizontally flipped inputs are a deterministic computation cache, with no labels or dataset-fitted parameters. This permits CPU execution within the available disk/memory budget. Checkpoints include optimizer state for epoch-boundary resume, deterministic epoch seeds, split/input/initializer signatures, and checksum-verified model state. Completed fits refuse mismatched configuration or input reuse. Three neural tests pass, including actual training, prefix-cache equivalence, frozen-parameter/BatchNorm checks and exact checkpoint prediction reproduction. Full experiment training is still pending.

## Nested evaluation and execution (implemented)

`src/eyelid_transfer_run.py` preserves the exact historical outer subject assignments, fits three inner folds for each local strategy, selects the strategy by pooled inner AP (ties A), and selects thresholds by maximizing inner sensitivity subject to specificity >=0.80 (ties specificity, then higher threshold). The public-only operating rule uses public validation labels only; it never uses local labels for fitting or threshold selection. Public validation metrics describe checkpoint/threshold selection, not an independent public test. Fixed 0.5 results are reported separately for every neural variant.

The additional LAB analysis fits new inner logistic models with training-only scalers and the unchanged six historical features. New outer models must reproduce original Experiment 1 probabilities within 1e-12, while their thresholds come only from inner OOF scores. This analysis is separately identifiable and does not update Experiment 1.

All comparisons include pooled and per-fold metrics, confusion counts, Wilson intervals for proportions, and 2,000 identical stratified subject bootstrap draws for ranking/F1/balanced accuracy and paired deltas against both prior experiments and the threshold-adjusted LAB comparator. Intervals are conditional on saved scores and do not include model-refit uncertainty or dependence from overlapping CV training sets.

CLI: `python -m src.eyelid_transfer_run prepare|run|verify --source-root /Users/madz/Documents/aima-screening --output <new ignored Experiment 3 directory>`. Preparation additionally requires `--public-root`; running requires `--weights` pointing to the cached, hash-verified MobileNetV3-Small ImageNet weights. Completed outputs are immutable and checksum-covered. Reproduction checks source and artifact hashes before loading, all 41 neural and 20 LAB checkpoints, train-only selections/scalers/weights, original outer scores, and fresh full-network equivalence to cached-prefix predictions. Ten Experiment 3 tests pass; full training follows the frozen settings with no outer-results tuning.

## Pre-training grouping correction

Final QA compared the legacy original-JPEG OpenCV pHash screen with the aligned-raster Pillow/scipy screen. The former flagged four public pairs and the latter zero; one eligible pair crossed the first public split. Attempt v2 was stopped before comparison and preserved with a checksum manifest. Input preparation now groups the union of both screens, and execution independently checks that neither screen was omitted. Corrected v3 keeps 146 public training subjects (54 anemia/92 non-anemia) and 37 validation subjects (10/27). Only verified color-managed tensors and frozen ImageNet prefix maps were reused; no public-trained or locally adapted weights were reused. No outer evaluation metrics informed this integrity correction.
